# Channel Management Software editorial review

Reviewed November 10, 2026 against the 15-page source PDF and public vendor materials. The guide keeps the source's material claims and comparison figures pending editorial approval. Listed rates are public reference points. Confirm entitlements and contract terms with the vendors before updating the copy.

| Platform and PDF pages | Pricing and licensing check | Capability or copy to review |
| --- | --- | --- |
| JazzHQ (pp. 2–3, 12) | The PDF says custom pricing. JazzHQ advertises free marketplace listings for vendors and does not publish a paid Partner OS or activation rate on the pages reviewed. Clarify which paid offering “Custom” covers and distinguish it from the free listing. [JazzHQ platform](https://www.jazzhq.ai/) | JazzHQ describes the marketplace, AI-native Partner OS, vendor advisory and activation support. The public homepage emphasizes AI-first vendors. Confirm the broader SaaS positioning, which support is included in paid packages, and which routine follow-ups are currently automated. [JazzHQ company and product history](https://www.jazzhq.ai/about-us) |
| ZINFI (pp. 4–5, 12) | The PDF lists Starter at $1,750/month for 100 partners and Professional at $2,850/month for up to 100. Current pricing cards advertise starting at $1,450 and $2,350/month respectively, billed annually, with setup fees of $3,480 and $5,640. The same page's package summary lists $2,250 and $3,500/month for up to 250 partners, matching the PDF's 250-partner amounts. Enterprise is custom. Ask ZINFI which partner counts the advertised starting rates cover before replacing either figure or the comparison-table price. [ZINFI UPM pricing](https://www.zinfi.com/pricing/) | The product materials support modular PRM, partner marketing, deal registration and incentives. The plan matrix lists SCORM+ and several marketing or incentive modules only on higher tiers. Confirm the plan and configuration required for certification-gated deal registration. The PDF's claim that users “unanimously” report complexity needs a review sample or narrower wording. [ZINFI PRM overview](https://www.zinfi.com/products/partner-relationship-management/overview/) · [ZINFI module matrix](https://www.zinfi.com/pricing/) |
| Salesforce Partner Cloud (pp. 5–6, 12) | The $25/member/month PRM and $50/member/month PEM prices match Salesforce's annual add-on terms. Salesforce also lists $10/login/month for PRM and $20/login/month for PEM, billed annually. Its add-on guide lists Enterprise, Unlimited and Agentforce 1 Sales as eligible. Channel Revenue Management has variable pricing. The pricing FAQ confirms there is no free trial and points to workshops or a sandbox. Label the comparison-table figure as member-based and note the base license and billing terms. [Salesforce Partner Cloud pricing](https://www.salesforce.com/sales/partner-relationship-management/pricing/) · [Salesforce add-on price list, p. 4](https://www.salesforce.com/en-us/wp-content/uploads/sites/4/documents/pricing/all-add-ons.pdf) | The correct name is **Agentforce**. Salesforce documents Flex Credit requirements and different template availability for agent features. It attributes conversation-based deal registration and updates to PRM for Slack. Distinguish that app from Slack Connect and specify any agent setup separately. [Salesforce Partner Cloud](https://www.salesforce.com/sales/partner-cloud/) · [PRM for Slack help](https://help.salesforce.com/s/articleView?id=sf.slack_apps_prm_for_slack_features.htm&language=en_US&type=5) |
| Channeltivity (pp. 6–7, 12) | The current pricing page describes tailored, non-seat-based quotes and gives no public base rate. An older vendor checklist says unlimited users. Confirm whether that term applies to all current contracts before promising it. [Channeltivity pricing](https://www.channeltivity.com/pricing/) · [Channeltivity buyer checklist](https://www-cdn-1.channeltivity.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/9-questions-before-buying-prm-channeltivity-ebook.pdf) | Native connectors are documented for named systems including Salesforce, HubSpot and Dynamics 365. Other CRMs and ERPs may need custom work, APIs or an integration service. The PDF's claim of native integrations for “any CRM, ERP, or business system” overstates the published scope. Verify Azure translation and supported social feeds against the current package before publishing. [Channeltivity platform](https://www.channeltivity.com/) · [Channeltivity integration help](https://help.channeltivity.com/support/solutions/articles/38033-what-integrations-are-available-in-channeltivity-) |
| Channelscaler (pp. 8–9, 12) | Its pricing page confirms a fixed quote per configured program for the contract term. It also advertises a starting figure of $50,000 per module annually. The vendor distinguishes a module from a program configured within a module. If adding the starting figure to the table, preserve both units and confirm how it applies to a quoted deployment. [Channelscaler pricing](https://channelscaler.com/pricing/) | The vendor confirms that PRM and channel program automation cover complex, multi-tier ecosystems. Allbound acquired Channel Mechanics in March 2024. The combined company announced the Channelscaler brand on May 30, 2025. Clarify the acquisition and branding dates separately. Confirm whether the order and cancellation forms described in the pros apply to the same product edition shown. [Channelscaler platform](https://channelscaler.com/platforms/) · [March 2024 announcement](https://channelscaler.com/resources/press-releases/channel-mechanics-joins-forces-with-allbound-to-create-a-market-leading-prm-platform-now-channelscaler/) · [May 2025 rebrand](https://channelscaler.com/resources/press-releases/allbound-and-channel-mechanics-unite-under-a-single-brand-channelscaler/) |
| Mindmatrix (pp. 9–10, 12) | The demo and BridgeAI product pages do not publish a list price. Confirm the quote structure, available bundles and whether BridgeAI is separately licensed. [Mindmatrix demo](https://www.mindmatrix.net/mindmatrix-request-demo/) · [Mindmatrix BridgeAI](https://www.mindmatrix.net/ecosystem-orchestration-platform-bridgeai/) | Mindmatrix documents BridgeAI's machine learning and generative AI, translation, prompt builder and usage reporting. It also documents Bridge as its PRM platform and BridgeAI as the AI capability powering it. Clarify whether “AI as a Service” describes a separately purchasable offering. Add a traceable source for the user quote about hiding functionality, or remove the quote with editorial approval. [Mindmatrix BridgeAI](https://www.mindmatrix.net/ecosystem-orchestration-platform-bridgeai/) · [Mindmatrix PRM](https://www.mindmatrix.net/next-gen-prm-software/) |
| Impartner (pp. 10–11, 13) | Impartner requests personalized quotes and lists distinct packages. Confirm which package includes CRM integration, learning management, Aimi, and incentive features. The public pricing page does not provide a starting price. [Impartner PRM pricing](https://impartner.com/request-prm-pricing/) | Vendor materials support PRM onboarding, pipeline and training. Impartner documents Aimi using partner context and program rules to answer questions and create records from natural-language instructions. Verify the package and configuration for any specific workflow rather than implying uniform availability. [Impartner PRM pricing and package features](https://impartner.com/request-prm-pricing/) · [Aimi product details](https://impartner.com/aimi-impartner-ai/) |
| Unifyr (Zift) (pp. 11–13) | Unifyr publishes Activate, Grow and Scale plan names and compares allowances by tier. The pricing page does not provide dollar amounts. Confirm quote terms and limits for partner organizations, individual partner users and AI credits. [Unifyr plans](https://www.unifyr.com/pricing) | The vendor confirms the Zift rebrand and documents partner marketing, deal registration, lead distribution and learning. Features such as campaign management, advanced deal workflows and MDF vary by tier. Confirm the exact builder, asset formats and localization scope in the relevant package. The PDF has an empty second caveat bullet on page 12 with no copy to transcribe. [Unifyr rebrand context](https://www.unifyr.com/newsroom/unifyr-achieves-9-years-of-clean-soc-2-type-ii-reports/) · [Unifyr solutions](https://www.unifyr.com/solutions/) · [Unifyr plan matrix](https://www.unifyr.com/pricing) |

The PDF includes several assertions about customer sentiment, ease of use and support quality. Vendor feature pages cannot independently substantiate those statements. The editor should attach specific review sources where the copy makes those claims or quotes a user. The guide already discloses JazzHQ's relationship to the comparison.
